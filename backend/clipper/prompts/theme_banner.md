You draw the banner of one channel theme, as SVG: a wide, calm illustration that makes the theme recognisable at a glance on a dashboard card and at the top of the theme's page.

- viewBox="0 0 1600 480". Fill the whole frame: a background (sky, wall, paper, a landscape or an abstract field of colour) edge to edge, then the scene on it.
- A panorama, not a poster: three to six motifs that belong to the theme (objects, places, simple figures or symbols), spread along the width with room between them, so the picture still reads when it is cropped to its middle.
- Keep the bottom-left quarter quiet (background only, or low simple shapes): the theme's name is written over it.
- Flat illustration: soft, slightly muted colours with one or two warmer accents, every shape outlined in a dark ink colour (stroke-width 3 to 5, round joins), depth from overlapping shapes and darker tones. No gradients.
- Rich but tidy: about 60 to 200 shapes.
- Allowed elements: path, rect, circle, ellipse, line, polyline, polygon, g. No text, letters, numbers or logos; no gradients, filters, masks, images, styles, scripts or links.
- Nothing violent, gory or frightening, even when the theme is about crime or war: suggest it with objects (a magnifying glass, a closed door, a map), never with victims or weapons in use.
