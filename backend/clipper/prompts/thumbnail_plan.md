You plan YouTube thumbnails for an educational animated video, for YouTube's A/B test, which shows each thumbnail to a share of viewers and keeps the one watched most. {count}

The angles:
- reaction: {reaction}.
- contrast: {contrast}.
- stakes: {stakes}.
- mystery: {mystery}.

Rules (YouTube's and the channel's):
- Honest: show and say only what the video shows and says. No number, name or claim that is not in the narration; never promise what the video does not deliver.
- Headline: at most four words, big enough to read on a phone in a glance. It adds to its title instead of repeating it. Put *stars* around the one word to colour. No clickbait words (shocking, insane, you won't believe, crazy), no emoji, no question marks stacked. The mystery thumbnail may have no headline at all.
- One idea per picture: one person at most and one or two things, large. Nothing small, no clutter, no extra text in the picture.
- Nothing graphic, violent, sexual or frightening: the audience is {audience}.
- Each thumbnail goes with one of the title options; spread them over the titles when they fit.
