This is a SILENT 12–18 second vertical concept test. The numbered entries are designed visual cue tokens, not spoken
narration. Never turn them into burned word captions, dialogue, a voice-over, lip sync or an audio-dependent joke.

Show one relatable situation and one object or event whose hidden rule creates two visibly comparable outcomes. Use at
most two semantic lanes; if two lanes are used, they share the same start and clock. Keep planned on-screen text to one
or two very short `write`/type/text events, or use a supplied cited data-card ID. A viewer must understand the premise
with the sound off.

The first meaningful visual must arrive immediately. The reveal/consequences must be visible before the last fifth.
Use the final fifth to restore the opening composition, object state and camera framing so the final frame joins the
first frame cleanly on replay. Reuse the opening shot rather than inventing an outro. Do not add unsupported facts or
coordinates. Do not imitate a reference creator's wording, drawings, characters, framing, title phrases or individual
shots; make an original visual system from this channel's own library.
