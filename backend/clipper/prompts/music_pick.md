{prompt:music_tone} Judge each track only from what you are told about it. A title that suggests singing (a song name, "feat.", "vocal", a cover) is a bad sign; "instrumental" in a title is a good one. Library tracks are already downloaded; online ones are free to use but CC BY ones need a credit line in the description.

No music is often the better choice: for a Short of under a minute that relies on the punchline and the sound effects, when every track clashes with the tone, or when a track would make a serious or sad passage feel flippant. Say so when it applies.
