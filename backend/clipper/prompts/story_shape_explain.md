{prompt:story_shape_common}

For `explain`, show a visible mechanism and a causal chain. Put evidence before the conclusion, name the intermediate
steps a viewer needs, and finish with a concrete payoff. Do not invent a before/after or A/B contrast merely to make the
mechanism feel dramatic.
