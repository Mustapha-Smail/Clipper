## Watching a video

To watch a reel, Short, TikTok or video file (what it says, what it shows, its caption,
its on-screen text), follow `.claude/skills/watch-video/SKILL.md`. In short:
`cd backend && uv run clipper watch "<URL>"`, then read `data/watch/<id>/digest.md`
and look at every image in its `sheets/` folder (open frames from `frames/` to read
small text) before you report.
